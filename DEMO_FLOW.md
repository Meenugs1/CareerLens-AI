# CareerLens Demo Flow

Target duration: 5-7 minutes. Start the backend on port 8000 and the frontend on port 5174 before the demo.

## 1. Job Explorer

- Click `Explore Jobs`.
- Enter role `Data Analyst`.
- Enter skills `Python, SQL`.
- Set experience to `0`, location to `Any`, work mode to `Any`, and job type to `Internship`.
- Click `Recommend Jobs`.
- Demonstrate the summary, ranked job cards, skill match, and internship filtering.
- Explain that recommendation ranking is the existing multi-factor dataset scoring model.

## 2. Market Intelligence

- Click `Market Intelligence`.
- Search `Data Analyst`.
- Demonstrate observed job count, locations, companies, skills, experience, salary records, and related titles.
- Explain that the figures describe listings represented in the current dataset.

## 3. Skill Gap

- Click `Skill Gap`.
- Enter role `Data Analyst`.
- Enter skills `Python, SQL`.
- Click `Analyze Skill Gap`.
- Demonstrate matching and missing observed skills and the two-skill coverage definition.

## 4. Career Simulator

- Click `Career Simulator`.
- Enter role `Data Analyst`.
- Enter current skills `Python, SQL`.
- Enter skill to add `Tableau`.
- Click `Run What-If`.
- Demonstrate before/after matching jobs and coverage change.

## 5. Career Switch

- Click `Career Switch`.
- Enter current role `Data Analyst`.
- Enter current skills `Python, SQL`.
- Enter target role `Data Engineer`.
- Click `Analyze Career Switch`.
- Demonstrate target-role skill overlap and missing observed skills without describing the result as a guarantee.

## 6. Career Path

- Click `Career Path`.
- Enter starting role `Data Analyst`.
- Click `Explore Career Path`.
- Demonstrate related roles, shared skills, additional observed skills, title similarity, and evidence text.
- Explain that the relationships are observed dataset similarities, not guaranteed progression paths.

## 7. What Can I Become?

- Click `What Can I Become?`.
- Enter `Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI`.
- Click `Analyze Skills`.
- Demonstrate analyzed jobs, matching jobs, role totals, match percentages, matching skills, and additional common skills.
- Explain that a job counts only when at least two normalized selected skills are present.

## 8. Company Intelligence

- Click `Company Intelligence`.
- Search `Accenture` and select the suggestion.
- Click `Analyze Company`.
- Demonstrate 8,193 represented jobs, grouped roles, locations, normalized skills, one salary-data record, and dataset-listed rating evidence.
- Search `Infosys` for a second represented company if time allows.
- Search `IBM` to demonstrate a company with no available salary records.
- Explain that company evidence does not represent complete current hiring activity.
